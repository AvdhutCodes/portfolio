"Woke at 5:30 AM. Read twenty pages of Seneca's Epistles with black coffee. Noticing how much modern productivity advice is just repackaged Stoicism with a subscription fee."

Fragments & Marginalia

  • On Reading Velocity: The greatest delusion is that skimming fifty summaries equals understanding one difficult paragraph. If your eyes aren't stopping, your brain isn't wrestling.
  • On Cinema & Sound: Watched Stalker by Tarkovsky again. The sound of train wheels clacking in the rain communicates more metaphysical weight than two hours of CGI explosions.
  • The Three Pillars: Reading feeds the imagination; cinema tests empathy; sound tunes the nervous system. The work is simply the synthesis of all three.
