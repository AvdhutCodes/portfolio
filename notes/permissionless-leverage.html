The Evolution of Leverage

"Forget rich versus poor, white-collar versus blue-collar. It's now leveraged versus unleveraged."

The Four Forms of Leverage

To understand how value is captured in the modern world, consider the four archetypes of leverage:

1. Labor (Oldest, Hardest)

Having other humans work for you. It requires tremendous politics, management overhead, and emotional friction. Everyone is trying to manage everyone else.

2. Capital (The 20th Century Engine)

Money working for money. It scales well, but requires permission from banks, venture funds, or inherited wealth.

3. Code (The Software Era)

Programs executing instructions round the clock without fatigue. An algorithm will run at 3 AM with zero salary and zero resentment.

4. Media & Writing (The Gutenberg Era Perfected)

Essays, podcasts, books, websites, and recordings. A note written in Obsidian and published to GitHub Pages can be read by 10,000 people simultaneously across six continents while you are asleep in bed.

       Permission Required?        Marginal Cost of Replication
Labor         YES                               HIGH
Capital       YES                               MEDIUM
Code          NO (Permissionless)               ZERO
Media         NO (Permissionless)               ZERO

The Bibliophile's Advantage
