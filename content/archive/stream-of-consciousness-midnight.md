## The Solitude Dividend

Why do bibliophiles and makers love late hours? It is not about sleep deprivation; it is about **psychological insulation**.
When society powers down, the atmospheric pressure of other people's anxieties lifts. You can sit with a book, a sketch, or a musical passage and exist purely in the moment.
