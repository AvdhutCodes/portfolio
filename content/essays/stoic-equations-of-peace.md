> [!quote] The Emperor's Reminder to Himself
> "When you wake up in the morning, tell yourself: The people I deal with today will be meddling, ungrateful, arrogant, dishonest, jealous, and surly. They are like this because they cannot distinguish good from evil. But I have seen the beauty of good, and the ugliness of evil..."

## The Private Notebook of the Master of the World

Consider who Marcus Aurelius was: the supreme emperor of the Mediterranean world. He had absolute authority over legions, treasuries, and human lives. Yet in the freezing dark of his military camp along the Danube, fighting off plagues and Germanic invasions, he did not write decrees of conquest.

He wrote a therapeutic journal to himself. He never meant for *Meditations* to be published.

## Three Stoic Equations

### 1. Suffering = Event × Resistance
The external event (the rainstorm, the insulting colleague, the market downturn) is neutral nature. The suffering is your internal judgment: *This should not be happening to me.*

### 2. Desire = A Contract You Make to Be Unhappy
Naval Ravikant phrased this with modern precision: *"Desire is a contract you make with yourself to be unhappy until you get what you want."* Pick at most one desire at a time; otherwise, you scatter your peace.

### 3. Freedom = Independence from the Opinions of Fools
Why do we love ourselves more than other people, but care more about their opinions than our own? When you stop seeking applause, you cannot be blackmailed.
