> [!quote] The Ancient Maxim
> "Perfection is achieved, not when there is nothing more to add, but when there is nothing left to take away." — Antoine de Saint-Exupéry

## The Bias Toward Addition

When human beings encounter friction, our default instinct is additive:
- If we feel unfocused, we download another productivity application.
- If a project is late, we hire more engineers.
- If our writing is weak, we add adjectives and complex jargon.

In almost all complex domains, addition increases fragility.

## The Power of Via Negativa

Nassim Taleb popularized the concept of *Via Negativa* (by way of denial or subtraction):
1. **In health**: Removing sugar, seed oils, and chronic sleep deprivation produces ten times more vitality than adding twenty exotic supplements.
2. **In writing**: Pruning superfluous words and passive voice strengthens an argument far more than embellishment.
3. **In thinking**: Avoiding stupidity is far easier than trying to be brilliant. As Charlie Munger said, *"It is remarkable how much long-term advantage people like us have gotten by trying to be consistently not stupid, instead of trying to be very intelligent."*

```
Addiction to Addition  -> Fragility, Noise, Overhead
Discipline of Subtraction -> Antifragility, Signal, Peace
```
