## The Masterpiece of Dynamic Range

In 2007, during the absolute nadir of the "Loudness Wars" where commercial pop CDs were brickwalled into distorted dynamic sludge, Radiohead and producer Nigel Godrich went in the exact opposite direction.

*In Rainbows* is celebrated by sound engineers not just for its songcraft, but for its **infinite acoustic breathing room**:

### 1. The Clapping Polymeter of *15 Step*
The track kicks off with a sharp 5/4 drum machine glitch loop recorded through an analog filter, accompanied by children cheering recorded at a London school, and Colin Greenwood’s warm, rounded bass line that anchors the entire groove.

### 2. The Holographic Soundstage of *Weird Fishes/Arpeggi*
Listen on open-back planar magnetic headphones or high-sensitivity studio monitors:
- Ed O’Brien and Jonny Greenwood’s interlocking Stratocasters pan symmetrically left and right.
- Phil Selway’s ghost notes on the snare dance like ripples on calm water.
- You can hear the physical wood resonance of the acoustic instruments.

> [!tip] Listening Setup
> Best experienced on analog vinyl or 24-bit/96kHz lossless FLAC. Dim the room lights and listen through uninterrupted.
