## The Physicality of Temple Church

To record the score, Hans Zimmer bypassed Hollywood scoring stages and moved his team to **Temple Church in London**, built in the 12th century by the Knights Templar.

The centerpiece is the 1927 Harrison & Harrison four-manual pipe organ:
- The low pedal notes vibrate at **16 Hz to 32 Hz**—sub-bass frequencies that human ears cannot hear directly, but which vibrate inside the human ribcage.
- The sound of the organist pumping air into the bellows was left deliberately in the mix, giving the instrument the sound of an astronaut breathing inside a helmet.

### *Mountains* and the 1.25-Second Metric
In the track *Mountains*, each audible tick is spaced at **1.25 seconds**. On Miller’s water planet, every tick represents an entire day passing on Earth.
