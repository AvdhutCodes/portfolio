## Pity over Might

In *The Lord of the Rings*, the ultimate defeat of Sauron does not come from a grand confrontation between archmages or kings.
- It was Bilbo’s pity in sparing Gollum in *The Hobbit* that preserved the thread of providence.
- It was Frodo’s quiet endurance across the ash heaps of Gorgoroth.
- It was Samwise Gamgee holding his master’s hand when water ran dry.

```
Power Corrupts          -> The One Ring

The Cure for Despair    -> Doing the next small duty
```
