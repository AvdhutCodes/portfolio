## Effort vs. Judgment

In an industrial economy, labor was linear. If you spent ten hours turning bolts on an assembly line, you produced roughly twice as much as five hours.

In an algorithmic, connected world, **leverage is nonlinear**.
- A trader who makes one right macro call creates millions of dollars in seconds.
- A designer who picks the right system architecture saves three years of technical debt.
- A writer who crystallizes an evergreen idea reaches millions without expending extra ink.

brute effort has been commoditized. **Judgment is scarce.**

## Where Judgment Originates

Where does genuine judgment come from?
It does not come from rushing or panic. It comes from:

1. **Clear thinking without ego.**
   When you don't care about looking smart, you see things as they actually are.
2. **First principles thinking.**
   Deconstruct problems into their most fundamental truths and reason upward from there.
3. **Deep domain intuition through immersion.**
   Reading broadly across physics, mathematics, history, and literature gives you a lattice of mental models.
